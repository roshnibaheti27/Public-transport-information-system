SmartTransit is a modern Public Transport Information System that helps users find buses, metro, trains, taxis and travel information from one place.

WHO WE ARE

We Make Public Transport Easier, Faster & Smarter

SmartTransit is designed to simplify public transportation by combining buses, trains, metro and taxi information into a single easy-to-use platform.

Our mission is to provide accurate route information, estimated arrival times, nearby stations and travel updates through a clean and modern interface.
